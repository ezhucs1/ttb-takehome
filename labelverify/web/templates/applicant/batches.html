{{ cola_rows or 60 }} approved labels fetched from TTB's public COLA registry, twenty of each class, many with back and neck panels. Twenty rows are a demonstration with known answers: values a person transcribed from the label, some then deliberately altered (wrong alcohol content, net contents, class or brand, a one-letter near miss) or left blank. The other forty carry the registry's own record: its class code and permit-holder name are not the label's wording, so those rows mostly come back as corrections needed, and the unpublished alcohol content and net contents as review items.
